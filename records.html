Mountaineering Knowledge Base (6 Modules)

Module 1
High-Altitude Hazards

Learn about AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness), HAPE, HACE, frostbite symptoms, and crossing crevasse zones safely.

Module 2
Emergency Shelters

Techniques for carving emergency snow caves, pitching 4-season geodesic tents, and deploying insulated bivy sacks.

Module 3
Essential Climbing Knots

Step-by-step knot guide: Figure-8 loop on a bight, Clove Hitch for anchors, Bowline, and Prusik friction hitch.

Module 4
Acclimatization Rules

The golden rule: "Climb high, sleep low". Never ascend more than 300 to 500 meters sleeping elevation per day.

Module 5
High-Altitude Nutrition

Carbohydrate-dense meals, electrolyte replenishment, and melting clean glacial snow for 4 to 5 liters of daily water.

Module 6
First Aid & Rescue

Emergency protocols: immediate descent, portable Gamow hyperbaric chambers, supplemental oxygen bottles, and helicopter signals.
