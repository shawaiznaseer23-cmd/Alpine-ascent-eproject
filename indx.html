Climber Preparation

Climber Fitness & Training Guidelines

Mandatory physical and cardiovascular preparation before attempting high-altitude summits

Aerobic Endurance

Minimum 4-6 months of Zone 2 endurance cardio. Long-distance trail running, weighted pack stair climbing (15-25 kg), and cycling for 60-90 minutes daily.

Target VO2 Max: 48+
Altitude Acclimatization

Adhering strictly to gradual ascent curves. Mandatory staging nights at intermediate camps (3,500m & 5,000m) with pulse-oximeter blood monitoring twice daily.

Rule: Climb High, Sleep Low
Technical Safety Drills

Mastering self-arrest with ice axe on 45-degree ice slopes, crampon front-pointing, mechanical ascender (Jumar) jugging, and crevasse rescue hauling pulleys.

Level: UIAGM Verified

High-Altitude Ethics

Leave No Trace & Clean Summit Pledge

Every Alpine Ascents summit expedition enforces strict environmental preservation. We pack out 100% of forward-camp waste, human bio-waste, and discarded oxygen cylinders from high zones to protect fragile mountain ecosystems.
