Club Membership FAQs

Everything you need to know about joining and training with Alpine Ascents

Yes! We have beginner developmental tracks starting with basic rope management, crampon walking on easy glaciers, and non-technical treks like Mount Kilimanjaro and Himalayan Base Camps. No prior experience is required for Level 1 treks.

All applicants joining technical summits must submit a physician-certified cardiovascular stress test, ECG clearance, and participate in our 12-week supervised conditioning syllabus with weekly fitness logs.

We provide all group safety hardware including technical dynamic ropes, snow anchors, satellite emergency transmitters, and expedition high-altitude tents. Personal gear (boots, down suits, harnesses) can be rented with a 35% member discount.

Every expedition is equipped with satellite telemetry communicating twice daily with mountain meteorological stations. Forward camps carry high-flow supplemental oxygen, Gamow hyperbaric chambers, and chartered SOS helicopter telemetry.
